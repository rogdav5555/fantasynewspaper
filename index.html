The Midnight Observatory Opens Its Doors After 73 Silent Years

A forgotten tower above the northern moors has begun glowing again, drawing stargazers, historians, and at least one extremely confused goat.
The observatory at midnight. Witnesses insist the stars were “moving sideways.”

For generations, the old observatory stood dark against the hills, its brass dome green with age. Last night, however, every window blazed with silver light and the great telescope turned of its own accord.

Professor Elian Voss, keeper of the nearby archive, says the event may be connected to a celestial alignment not recorded since the founding of the old kingdoms.

“The building was not abandoned. It was waiting.” — Professor Voss

Visitors are advised to keep a respectful distance until the Observatory Council determines whether the staircase is, in fact, supposed to be floating.
